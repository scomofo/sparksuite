# Piano Highway Paintover Brief

Use `piano_highway_v3.png` as the base and treat this as an overpaint, not a redraw.

## Goal

Keep the existing perspective and gameplay readability, but make the Piano runway feel more premium, more instrument-specific, and less like a generic game lane.

## Base Asset

- `sparkgame/assets/highway/piano_highway_v3.png`

## What To Keep

- exact runway silhouette
- existing perspective and vanishing point
- current in-engine readability
- overall center-lane calmness

## What To Change

### Materials

- shift the surface toward polished stage flooring or a lacquered dark recital-stage material
- reduce the generic “game texture” feel
- aim for a more luxurious concert-floor finish

### Lane Guidance

- reduce obvious lane-striping
- replace it with subtle panel seams, faint geometric divisions, or soft illuminated insets
- keep the guidance readable, but not literal

### Color

- keep cool blue as the primary edge-light color
- add restrained warm gold accents only where helpful
- avoid strong amber bands across the center path

### Depth

- add slightly richer center shading and floor gradient
- soften the far end into faint haze so it sits better into the background plate
- preserve the current perspective without exaggerating it

### Piano Identity

- do **not** add literal keyboard keys
- do **not** add black/white key patterns
- suggest Piano through elegance, spacing, symmetry, and refined surface geometry

## What To Avoid

### Road-Like Semantics

- no dashed center lines
- no traffic-lane markings
- no asphalt texture
- no painted road dividers

### Over-Literal Instrument Cues

- no full keyboard graphic
- no obvious keybed motif
- no note icons or music symbols

### Visual Noise

- no strong checkerboard/grid
- no busy geometric clutter
- no bright center hotspot
- no dense reflections in the hit-line zone

## Target Look

Think:

- premium recital-stage runway
- dark lacquered performance floor
- subtle architectural lighting
- elegant and minimal
- readable first, stylish second

## Paintover Checklist

1. Keep the outer runway edges and perspective locked.
2. Repaint the central floor with smoother, richer dark material.
3. Replace strong lane markers with faint panel seams.
4. Add soft blue edge glow.
5. Add very light warm accents near edges or panel joints.
6. Soften the far end into atmosphere.
7. Verify the center path still reads cleanly under falling notes.

## One-Sentence Art Brief

Overpaint this existing Piano highway surface into a premium dark recital-stage runway with subtle geometric panel seams, soft blue edge lighting, restrained warm accents, and no road markings or literal keyboard graphics.
